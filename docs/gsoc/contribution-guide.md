# Getting Started with Google Summer of Code (GSoC)

## About the Program

### Welcome to Google Summer of Code

[Google Summer of Code](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/) has been running every summer since 2005, with the intention of bringing more students into open source.

Open source projects apply as mentor organizations and if they are accepted, students send proposals to them to work on a few months' long project. Projects can be planned out by the organizations in advance or can be proposed by students.

Google pays the contributors, not the organizations they work with. Beginning in 2022, Google opened the program to newcomers to open source who are 18 years and older.

You can read more about the format of the program and its goals [here](https://google.github.io/gsocguides/mentor/).

## Keploy X GSoC

We at Keploy are excited to announce that Keploy is among the mentoring organizations from India 🇮🇳! If you are looking to participate in GSoC, Keploy offers several projects for you to choose from.

Keploy is looking for motivated contributors who are passionate about open source software and interested in gaining valuable experience in the field. We welcome contributors from all over the world with diverse backgrounds and skill sets to participate.

## How to Get Started

To get started with GSoC and Keploy, follow these steps:

1. If you are new to Keploy, try setting up Keploy in your local machine and running one (or more) of the sample applications. You can refer the [docs](#docs).
2. You can then check out the [projects](#current-gsoc-projects) that are selected for the current GSoC year and try completing the tasks in the task list. Remember, the tasks will act as a selection criteria for shortlisting candidates.
3. You can connect with the mentors over [Slack](https://keploy.io/slack) to clear any of your doubts.
4. Submit your proposal and that's it!

## Timeline

Google Summer of Code generally follows an annual schedule:

- **January – February**: Organization applications open and mentoring organizations are announced.
- **March**: Contributor applications open.
- **April – May**: Results and accepted projects are announced.
- **May – August**: Coding takes place over the summer.

For the exact dates and deadlines for the current year, check the official [Google Summer of Code Timeline](https://developers.google.com/open-source/gsoc/timeline).

## Conclusion

Participating in GSoC with Keploy is a great opportunity to gain valuable experience in open source software development. We look forward to receiving your proposals and working with you on our projects.
